[Surge] Validation & Reconciliation Framework for Historic Transactions
Summary
Repeatable framework for validating loaded historic transactions against source data. Includes reconciliation rules, tolerance thresholds, discrepancy reporting, and audit-ready output confirming data completeness and accuracy across AP, AR, and GL.
Business Value
Loading transactions without a validation framework creates risk — customers cannot confirm whether the loaded data is complete and accurate, and auditors have no evidence trail. A repeatable reconciliation framework gives customers and their auditors confidence that the migration is accurate, and gives delivery teams a standard process they can execute on every engagement.
Scope
Deliverable	Priority	Details
Reconciliation rules	P0	Define standard reconciliation rules for AP, AR, and GL (record counts, balance totals, key field matching)
Tolerance thresholds	P0	Configurable tolerance thresholds for acceptable discrepancies
Discrepancy reporting	P0	Automated reporting of discrepancies between source and target data
Audit-ready output	P0	Generate audit-ready reconciliation reports confirming completeness and accuracy
Acceptance Criteria
Reconciliation framework executes against loaded AP, AR, and GL data
Discrepancies are reported with source/target comparison details
Tolerance thresholds are configurable per engagement
Audit-ready reports are generated automatically
Framework is documented and repeatable across engagements
